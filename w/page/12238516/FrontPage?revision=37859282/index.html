Welcome to the OAuth wiki

 

  • The goal of this wiki is to aggregate code, resources, documentation and research in the interest of developing a generic authentication protocol for desktop applications, similar to FlickrAuth.

 

  • We currently have a mailing list, a website and a number of other support properties.
